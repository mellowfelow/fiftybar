Who We Are

WE ARE HERE TO UNLEASH THE SPIRIT OF AMERICAN CRAFTSMANSHIP

Fifty Bar Vape was born from a simple but powerful idea: American vapers deserve an American-made product. In a market flooded with foreign-manufactured disposables, we set out to build something different — something our country could be proud of.

Every single Fifty Bar device is built and filled right here in the United States of America. Our e-liquid is crafted with American-sourced ingredients, our devices are assembled at our US facility, and every quality check is performed by Americans who take pride in what they make.

We believe it's time to bring home something truly genuine. That belief drives every flavor we develop, every device we build, and every order we ship.
