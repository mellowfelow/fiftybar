Last updated: May 2026

Overview

Fifty Bar Vape ("we," "us," or "our") operates thefiftybar.org. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website and place orders.

Information We Collect

When you place an order or contact us, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and payment method preference. We do not store credit card numbers or process payments directly — payments are made through third-party services (Apple Pay, Cash App, Chime, or cryptocurrency).

We also collect standard web analytics data through cookies and similar technologies, including pages visited, time on site, and general geographic location (country/state level only).

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to: process and fulfill your orders; communicate with you about your order status; respond to customer service inquiries; send newsletters if you opt in (you can unsubscribe at any time); and improve our website and product offerings.

Information Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share limited information with shipping carriers to fulfill your order, and with service providers (such as Formspree for form processing) who assist us in operating our website, subject to confidentiality agreements.

Age Verification

Our products contain nicotine and are intended for adults 21 years of age and older only. By using our website, you confirm that you are of legal age to purchase tobacco/vaping products in your jurisdiction. We take age verification seriously and reserve the right to cancel any order we believe to have been placed by a minor.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We encourage you to use strong, unique passwords and to contact us immediately if you believe your account information has been compromised.

Cookies

We use session cookies (for age gate verification) and analytics cookies to understand how visitors use our site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some site functionality may be affected.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at info@thefiftybar.org.

Contact Us
