Fifty Bar Strawberry Cereal Donut Milk: Review & vs. Blueberry BCDM

The Blueberry version made Fifty Bar famous in the dessert category. Now the strawberry variant is here — and it may actually be the better one. Here's the full breakdown.

The Fifty Bar Strawberry Cereal Donut Milk is the answer to one of the most common questions we hear: "Is there a strawberry version of the Blueberry Cereal Donut Milk?" The answer is yes — and after testing both head to head, the strawberry variant brings enough of its own character to stand completely on its own.

What Is Fifty Bar Strawberry Cereal Donut Milk?

Like its blueberry sibling, this is a layered dessert and cereal hybrid from the Black Series — Fifty Bar's 20,000-puff premium dessert collection, formulated by Beard Vape Co. in California. The concept: a glazed donut, a bowl of strawberry fruit cereal, and whole cold milk, all blended into a single vape experience.

Full Taste Profile

Inhale

The inhale opens with ripe strawberry — not the artificial candy-strawberry you find in generic disposables, but a closer-to-real-fruit sweetness that reads as sun-warmed berries. Underneath it, the first suggestion of a glazed donut base starts to emerge, adding a buttery, doughy warmth that grounds the fruit note.

Mid-Vape

This is where the cereal element comes forward. A lightly crunchy, sugary cereal texture layers over the strawberry — think frosted strawberry puffs in a bowl — and the overall sweetness peaks here. It's rich without being cloying, largely because the milk note starts to balance it.

Exhale

The exhale is defined by cold milk. It's the same trick that makes the Blueberry version so effective — the dairy note on the back end cools and softens everything that came before, giving the draw a clean, creamy finish. The strawberry lingers lightly but doesn't overstay.
