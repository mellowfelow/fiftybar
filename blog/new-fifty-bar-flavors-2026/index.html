New Fifty Bar Flavors 2026: Gummy Whip, OMG Smoothie and Kiberry Chew Reviewed

Three of the most talked-about new additions to the Fifty Bar lineup. Here is a full review of each — what they taste like, where they sit in the catalog, and which one to try first.

Every time Fifty Bar drops a new flavor, the community pays attention. The brand's track record of USA-formulated, Beard Vape Co.-backed e-liquids means new arrivals rarely disappoint. In 2026, three new flavors have generated the most buzz: Gummy Whip, OMG Smoothie, and Kiberry Chew. Here is a full review of each, plus our recommendation on which to prioritize.

Review 1 of 3 - New Flavor

Fifty Bar Gummy Whip

Gummy Whip is a candy-dessert hybrid — a blend of soft gummy candy sweetness with a light whipped cream finish. It sits in a category Fifty Bar has not heavily explored before: the candy-meets-dairy space. The result is surprisingly balanced. The gummy fruit note (a blend of tropical mixed candy, similar to gummy bears) comes first, bright and chewy. The whipped cream element enters on the mid-vape and softens the sweetness without making the flavor heavy. The exhale is clean and creamy.

Review 2 of 3 - New Flavor

Fifty Bar OMG Smoothie

The name says it all: Orange, Mango, Guava. OMG Smoothie is a thick, tropical fruit smoothie blend that lives in the White Series alongside Baja Slushie and is quickly establishing itself as a fan favorite. The orange note is fresh and bright on the inhale. Mango dominates the mid-vape — rich, ripe, and true to the fruit rather than candy-mango artificial. The guava arrives on the exhale as a slightly floral, sweet finish that makes the whole profile feel genuinely tropical and refreshing. Unlike many fruit blends that blur together into a single "tropical punch" wall, OMG Smoothie keeps its three elements distinct and identifiable throughout the draw.

Review 3 of 3 - New Flavor

Fifty Bar Kiberry Chew

Kiberry Chew is the most unique of the three new arrivals. The name suggests kiwi and berry, and the flavor delivers exactly that — but with a chewy candy texture as the conceptual base. The kiwi note is tart and distinct, not buried under berry sweetness. The berry element (a blend of strawberry and mixed berry) supports the kiwi rather than overpowering it. The "chew" in the name refers to the candy-like mouthfeel of the vapor — thick, slightly sticky-sweet, with the texture of a fruit chew candy. It is genuinely addictive and stands alone in the Fifty Bar catalog as the closest thing to a tart fruit candy vape the brand currently offers.
