Fifty Bar Baja Slushie Review: The Tropical Slush You Have Been Missing

The Fifty Bar Baja Slushie has a reputation that precedes it. Among the White Series lineup, it is the flavor that gets mentioned in the same breath as Baja Burst and OMG Smoothie as a must-have — but with a character that is distinctly its own. This is the full review: taste profile, consistency, comparisons, and who it is built for.

What Is the Fifty Bar Baja Slushie?

Baja Slushie is a tropical lime citrus slush flavor from the White Series. Think of the icy, tangy blue slush drinks you find at convenience stores — citrusy, cold, and impossibly refreshing — translated into a premium disposable vape format using Beard Vape Co.'s California-formulated e-liquid.

Full Taste Profile

Inhale

The first thing you notice is a clean, sharp lime burst — not the artificial sourness of lesser disposables, but a genuine citrus note that sits at the front of the palate. A tropical fruit undertone (mango and a hint of passion fruit) gives it depth without softening the citrus edge.

Mid-Vape

The slush character comes through mid-draw: a cooling sensation that mimics the icy texture of a frozen drink. This is the defining moment of the Baja Slushie — the cold hit that separates it from straightforward citrus flavors. It's refreshing in a way that fruit-only profiles rarely achieve.

Exhale

The exhale is clean, bright, and slightly sweet — the tropical fruit note returns briefly before the cooling fades. There is no chemical aftertaste, no medicinal edge. Just a clean finish that makes you want the next draw immediately.

Baja Slushie is one of the very few Fifty Bar flavors that earns legitimate all-day vape status. The icy finish resets your palate after every draw, so it never gets heavy.

Who Should Try Fifty Bar Baja Slushie?

  • All-day vape seekers — the palate-resetting cool finish makes it genuinely sustainable across a full day
  • Citrus and fruit drink fans — if you love Gatorade Frost, blue slushy drinks, or lime-based anything, this is the flavor
  • Vapers switching from menthol — the cooling element gives a similar throat satisfaction without a pure mint profile
  • Hot weather vaping — the icy character makes this especially satisfying in warm conditions
