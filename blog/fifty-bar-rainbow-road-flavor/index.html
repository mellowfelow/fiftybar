Taste Profile & Flavor Notes

The rainbow road fifty bar flavor is best described as a rotating carnival of fruit candy notes. Here's what you'll notice on the draw:

Inhale

The first thing that hits is a burst of tropical mixed fruit — think mango and passion fruit leading the charge, with a bright strawberry note riding alongside. It's sweet but not one-dimensional; you can detect individual fruit layers rather than a generic "fruit punch" wall of sugar.

Mid-Vape

As the vapor settles, a citrus undertone — lime and a hint of orange — surfaces and lifts the flavor. This is what keeps Rainbow Road from feeling heavy. The citrus cuts through the sweetness and gives the blend an airy, refreshing quality.

Exhale

The exhale is where the candy character fully arrives. A slight sugary finish — reminiscent of sour fruit candy coated in a thin sugar shell — closes out each draw cleanly. There's no lingering chemical aftertaste, which is the Beard Vape Co. difference at work.

Rainbow Road is the flavor for vapers who want something loud and fruity but layered enough to hold their attention across 20,000 puffs.
